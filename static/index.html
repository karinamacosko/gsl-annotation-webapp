Translating English into Ghanaian Sign Language (GSL) grammar

Please translate the sentences to Ghanaian Sign Language grammar. Do not write the English words in English order. Instead, write the signs you would produce, in the order you would sign them, using the names of signs from the GSL dictionary. Written forms of signs are always written in CAPITAL LETTERS.

Examples

EnglishGSL gloss
What is your name?YOUR NAME WHAT?
I am going to school tomorrow.TOMORROW ME GO SCHOOL
Where do you live?YOU LIVE WHERE?
I don't understand.ME UNDERSTAND NOT
My sister is a nurse in Kumasi.MY SISTER NURSE KUMASI

How to enter tokens

  • Start typing a sign; matching dictionary signs appear. Tap one (or use the arrow keys and Enter) to add it.
  • fs-"ACCRA" — if a word should be fingerspelled, choose fs- and type the letters. It becomes one token, e.g. fs-"Example".
  • SIGN_NOT_FOUND — if a word has no sign in the dictionary, choose SIGN_NOT_FOUND. You may suggest a sign; it stays highlighted as a different kind of token.
  • Tap × on a token to remove it, or use ‹ › to reorder. Backspace in the empty box removes the last token.
  • Before submitting, rate your confidence from 1 (unsure) to 3 (very confident).
